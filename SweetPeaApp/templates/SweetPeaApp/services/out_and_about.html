Supportive transport and companionship for everyday independence

At Sweet Pea Home Help, we believe that staying active and connected to your community is essential for happiness and wellbeing — no matter your age. Our Out & About service helps older adults and those needing a little extra support to get where they want to go, safely and confidently.

Whether it’s a trip to the shops, a doctor’s or hospital appointment, a visit to friends, or simply a walk and a coffee, our friendly companions are here to make every outing enjoyable and stress-free.

We offer door-to-door transport and accompaniment, ensuring you have someone reliable by your side the whole time. You’ll have the freedom to stay social, attend your favourite activities, and keep your independence — without worrying about getting there and back.

The Benefits of our Out & About Services

  • Safe, reliable transport for appointments, errands, and social outings.
  • Friendly companionship for confidence and reassurance.
  • Encouragement to stay active and connected within your community.
  • Tailored support to suit your personal routine and needs.

At Sweet Pea Home Help, it’s your time to enjoy — and we’re here to make every journey a little brighter.
