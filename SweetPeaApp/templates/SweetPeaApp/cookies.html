Cookie Policy

Last updated: February 2026

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites function correctly and provide information about how the site is used.

How We Use Cookies

Sweet Pea Home Help Ltd uses cookies to:

  • Ensure the website functions properly
  • Remember your cookie preferences
  • Understand how visitors use our website (with your consent)

Types of Cookies We Use

1. Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to operate correctly. They do not require your consent.

We use an essential cookie (or local storage setting) to remember your cookie preferences.

2. Analytics Cookies (Google Analytics)

With your permission, we use Google Analytics to collect anonymised information about how visitors use our website.

This may include:

  • Pages visited
  • Time spent on pages
  • Device type and browser
  • General geographic region (not precise location)

This helps us improve our website and understand what information is most useful.

Analytics cookies are only set if you click “Accept” on our cookie banner. If you click “Reject”, no analytics cookies will be used.

You can read Google’s privacy policy here:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

Contact Form Processing

Our enquiry form is processed using Web3Forms. While this does not set tracking cookies for marketing purposes, form submissions are transmitted securely to our business email account.

You can view Web3Forms’ privacy policy here:
https://web3forms.com/privacy

Managing Your Cookie Preferences

When you first visit our website, you are asked whether you wish to accept or reject analytics cookies.

You can change your preference at any time by clearing your browser cookies or by using our cookie settings link (if available in the footer).

How Long Cookies Are Stored

Essential cookies remain for as long as necessary to remember your preferences.

Google Analytics cookies are set according to Google’s standard retention periods.
