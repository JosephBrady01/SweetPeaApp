Why choose our family-run home help service in the Vale of Glamorgan and Cardiff?

At Sweet Pea Home Help, we provide compassionate, reliable home care support for individuals and families across the Vale of Glamorgan and Cardiff.

Whether you need help with daily tasks, companionship, shopping, or support getting out and about, our friendly local team is here to make life easier and more comfortable in your own home, offering a more personal alternative to traditional home care.

As a small, family-run service, we offer something many larger providers can’t match-consistency. You’ll always see familiar faces, building trusted relationships over time, without a revolving door of different carers.

We pride ourselves on delivering personalised home care support through companionship and help with everyday living and promoting independence, dignity, and peace of mind.

Choosing Sweet Pea Home Help means choosing a trusted, community-based service that truly cares. We’re here when you need us supporting families across Barry, Cowbridge, Penarth, Llantwit Major, Cardiff and the surrounding areas, with flexible, personalised support tailored to your individual needs.

About Us

We are Andy and Sarah, a husband and wife team based in the Vale Of Glamorgan, who are dedicated to helping you be as independent as possible. We have three grown up girls, one of whom has a severe learning disability, and we know personally how hard it can be to find someone you trust to look after your loved ones.

We made the decision to set up Sweet Pea Home Help as we know first hand how difficult it can be to access the right support. Following the sad loss of Sarah’s mum in 2017 we struggled to obtain the extra support her Dad needed as we could not be there everyday.

And if you were wondering why we are called Sweet Pea, it was Sarah’s Mum’s favourite flower.

About Our Service

Sweet Pea Home Help provides companionship and practical home care support to help older people and families live well and independently in their own homes across the Vale of Glamorgan and Cardiff. We specialise in lifestyle support and meaningful company, offering a more personal alternative to traditional home care services.

We focus on companionship and everyday living support rather than personal care (such as washing, dressing or medical services). If more complex care is ever needed, we are happy to guide families towards an appropriate regulated provider.
